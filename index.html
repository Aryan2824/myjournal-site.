myJournal

A private, hand-bound journal that lives on your phone.

  • Write on ruled paper with handwriting fonts, lists and sketches.
  • Keep photos, voice memos, moods and the places you were.
  • Browse by timeline, calendar and map.
  • Optional App Lock with fingerprint or screen PIN.
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive (only files the app creates).
