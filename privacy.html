myJournal – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 November 2026

myJournal is a personal journaling app for Android. Your journal is private: it is stored on your phone, and (only if you choose) backed up to your own Google Drive. We do not run servers that receive your journal, and we do not sell, share, or analyse your data. The app contains no ads and no analytics.

What the app stores on your phone

  • Your journal entries: titles, text, moods, tags, dates.
  • Photos, sketches and voice notes you attach (copied into the app's private storage).
  • The place of an entry (coordinates and an address name), if you add one.
  • Settings such as theme, layout and whether App Lock is on.

This data stays on your device. It is removed when you delete entries or uninstall the app.

Google Drive backup (optional)

If you choose Continue with Google, the app asks Google for:

  • Access to files it creates in your Drive (drive.file). myJournal saves each entry as a Word document in a myJournals folder in your Google Drive. It cannot see or change any other files in your Drive.
  • Your email address, name and profile photo, used only to show which account is connected on your profile page inside the app.

This data goes directly between your phone and Google. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. You can disconnect at any time in the app (Profile → Disconnect Google Drive) and revoke access at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already backed up remain in your Drive until you delete them.

Google API Services: myJournal's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Permissions

  • Location – only to tag an entry with where you wrote it, when you allow it.
  • Microphone – only while you record a voice note.
  • Photos / camera – only to attach the pictures you choose.
  • Fingerprint / screen lock – only for the optional App Lock; handled by Android, the app never sees your fingerprint or PIN.
  • Internet – for Google Drive backup and for loading map tiles.

Maps

Maps in the app are drawn with OpenStreetMap tiles. To show a map, your phone downloads map images for the area being viewed from an OpenStreetMap tile server, which (like any website) receives your IP address. Your journal content is never sent.

Exports you share

If you use Export Journal or Share, the app creates a file or text and hands it to the app you pick (email, Files, etc.). What happens next is up to you and that app.

Children

myJournal is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect their data.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new date.
